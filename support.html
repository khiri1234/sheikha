Sheikha HQ – Support

Sheikha HQ is the head-office app of Sheikha Textiles. It shows live sales, branches, shifts and Z reports from the shop computers, and alerts head office when something needs attention.

Contact

Email sheikhatextiles@gmail.com. We reply within two working days.

Common questions

Who can sign in? Only the Sheikha Textiles head-office login. Branch staff use the POS on the branch computer.

Notifications don't arrive. Open More → Notifications, allow notifications and tap "Send test notification". The screen shows which step is missing.

The numbers look old. Pull down on the Dashboard to refresh. Branches that were offline upload their sales automatically when they reconnect.

I lost my phone. Change the head-office password straight away, then sign in on the new phone.
