Privacy Policy

Sheikha HQ iPhone app and Sheikha Textiles POS · Last updated 4 October 2026

Sheikha HQ is the head-office app of Sheikha Textiles LLC, United Arab Emirates. It shows the company's own sales, shifts and branch information to authorised head-office staff. It is not a consumer app and does not sell anything to the public.

What the app uses

  • Sign-in email – the head-office email address, used only to sign in (Google Firebase Authentication).
  • Push notification token – a code Apple gives this iPhone, stored so the company's alerts (shift closed, cash differences, voids, daily summary) reach it. It is removed when you sign out.
  • Business records – sales, invoices, shifts, cashier names and fabric prices of Sheikha Textiles, created by the shop computers. They are company records, not personal data about app users.
  • On this iPhone only – your choices for appearance, Face ID lock and the dashboard period. Face ID is handled by iOS; the app never sees your face data.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no tracking and no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) in a Google Cloud data centre in the Middle East (region me-central1), and sent over encrypted connections. Push notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service. Access is limited by security rules: each branch login sees only its own branch, and only the head-office login can use this app.

How long data is kept

Sales and shift records are kept as long as Sheikha Textiles needs them for its business and for UAE VAT record-keeping. Push tokens are deleted when you sign out or when they stop working.

Your choices

You can turn notifications off in iPhone Settings or in the app under More → Notifications, and sign out at any time under More. Accounts are created by Sheikha Textiles, not in the app; to have an account or its data removed, contact us below.
